One in five patients in the US speaks a language other than English at home. For many of them, calling the clinic means waiting for an interpreter line or giving up. Starting today, Halden answers them in their own language.

## How it works

The agent listens to the first few seconds of the call and picks up the caller’s language on its own. There is no “press 2 for Spanish” menu and no transfer.

- More than 30 languages, including Spanish, Mandarin, Arabic and Vietnamese
- Switches mid-call if the caller changes language
- Confirmation texts are sent in the caller’s language

## What stays the same

Your scripts, hand-off rules and booking logic don’t change. You write them once in English and Halden follows them in every language, so there is nothing new for your team to maintain.

<Quote cite="Front-desk lead, community health centre">“Half our callers prefer Spanish. Now they get booked on the first call instead of waiting for a call-back.”</Quote>

## Summaries your team can read

Every call summary and transcript is shown to your staff in English, with the original transcript one click away. When a call is handed to a person, the summary notes the caller’s language so the right staff member can pick it up.

## Turning it on

Multi-language answering is on for every plan. You can limit it to the languages your clinic supports in Settings, under Languages.
