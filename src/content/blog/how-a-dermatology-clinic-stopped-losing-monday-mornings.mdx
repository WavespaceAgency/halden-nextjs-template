Every Monday at 8 a.m., Brightwater’s phones lit up. Weekend voicemails, new bookings and refill requests all landed at once, and two front-desk staff could only answer one call at a time.

## The Monday problem

By 10 a.m. the voicemail box was full. Patients who couldn’t get through booked elsewhere, and the team spent the afternoon calling people back instead of helping the people in front of them.

<Quote cite="Dr. Lena Ortiz, Brightwater Dermatology">“Our voicemail went quiet in the first week. Patients get booked while they’re still on the phone.”</Quote>

## What we set up

In the first week our team connected Halden to their EHR, loaded provider schedules and visit types, and wrote scripts with the practice manager.

- Answer every call in one ring, 24/7
- Book new and returning patients straight into the EHR
- Send sensitive or clinical calls to staff with a summary
- Text a confirmation after every booking

## The rules that mattered

The biggest win wasn’t the AI. It was deciding up front which calls should always go to a person: new symptoms, billing disputes and anything about a minor.

## What to copy

Start with your busiest hour, not your whole week. Write the hand-off rules before the scripts. And review ten real calls every Friday for the first month.
