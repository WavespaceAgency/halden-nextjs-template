Referrals still arrive by fax at most specialist clinics. Someone has to read each one, type it in and call the patient – and while the stack waits, patients book somewhere else.

## What’s new

Halden now connects to your eFax inbox. When a referral arrives, it reads the form, pulls out the details and gets the patient booked.

- Reads patient details, reason for referral and the referring provider
- Matches the referral to the right provider and visit type
- Calls or texts the patient to book the first visit

## You stay in control

Anything unclear – a missing date of birth, a handwritten note it can’t read, an urgent flag – goes to your team with the fax attached. Nothing is booked without the rules you approved.

<Quote cite="Referral coordinator, cardiology practice">“The fax pile used to take a full morning. Now I only see the ones that need me.”</Quote>

## Getting started

Referral reading is available on the Growth and Enterprise plans. Connect your eFax account in Settings, under Integrations, and choose which visit types can be booked automatically.
