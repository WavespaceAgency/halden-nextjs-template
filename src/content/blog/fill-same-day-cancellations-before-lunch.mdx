A cancellation at 9 a.m. is an empty chair at 11 unless someone moves fast. Most front desks don’t have time to work through a waitlist by phone while the lobby fills up.

## Keep a waitlist that is actually used

Halden asks every caller who can’t get the slot they want whether they’d like an earlier one. Those patients go onto a waitlist with their preferred provider, times and visit type.

- Ask on every booking call, not only when the schedule is full
- Store the provider, visit type and times that work
- Remove patients automatically once they are booked

## Text the right patients first

When a slot opens, Halden texts the best matches first – same provider, same visit type, nearest in time – and books the first patient who replies. Everyone else gets a short “this one’s gone” message.

## What to copy

Set a cut-off – we suggest two hours before the visit – so patients have time to get there. And keep the text short: the time, the provider and a one-word reply to confirm.
