## Information we collect

We collect the information clinics give us during setup and the information patients share on calls, texts and chats handled by Halden.

- Account details such as name, work email and clinic name
- Call recordings, transcripts and booking details
- Usage data like log-ins, settings changes and device type

## How we use information

We use information only to run and improve the service for the clinic that owns it. We never use it for advertising.

- Answer, route and book patient calls
- Send booking confirmations and reminders
- Create call summaries and dashboards for your team

## Protected health information

Patient data is handled under a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) and in line with HIPAA.

- We never sell patient data
- We never use one clinic’s data to train models for another
- Only trained, authorised staff can access PHI

## Sharing and sub-processors

We share data only with vetted sub-processors we need to deliver the service. Each one is bound by a BAA.

- Cloud hosting and secure storage
- Telephony and SMS delivery
- EHR and scheduling integrations your clinic turns on

## Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based and every action is logged.

- Recordings are kept for 90 days by default
- Clinics can set a shorter or longer retention period
- Deleted data is removed from backups within 30 days

## Your rights and choices

Clinics stay in control of their data. Patients can contact their clinic about information held about them.

- Export or delete your clinic’s data at any time
- Ask us to correct inaccurate account details
- Email [privacy@example.com](mailto:privacy@example.com) with any request
